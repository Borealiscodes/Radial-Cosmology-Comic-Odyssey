## 🚀 Why This Project Is Ambitious

Rather than building a single-mechanic toy, **`Radial-Cosmology-Comic-Odyssey`** weaves **four distinct engineering layers** into one zero-dependency browser experience:

1. **Four-Act Radial Comic Panel Painting (`Zen-AI-Design-Architecture`):**  
   Instead of static rectangular comic boxes, each Act unfolds as an interactive **concentric mandala panel** that the player paints alive by gliding and emitting harmonic chimes:
   * **Act I — *Nascence* (Golden Dunes):** Minimalist ochre horizon, solitary traveler ribbon, and warm sub-bass cello drones.
   * **Act II — *The Bridge & Sunken City* (Interleaved Currents):** Nine chromatic *Navarasa* threads weave in sealed adjacency alongside plucked harp arpeggios.
   * **Act III — *The Tower* (Ascending Spiral):** Upward rotational currents and airy flute harmonics lift the traveler through crystalline snow-mandalas.
   * **Act IV — *Apotheosis* (Summit Mandala):** Full 16-sector radial symmetry blooms around the central pillar with a resonant choir chord.
2. **Spectral Soundtrack Decomposition (`Cosmic-Ballet-Traversal` + *Journey* Motifs):**  
   Splits procedural WebAudio synthesis into **four live spectral frequency bands** (`Cello Drone`, `Harp Arpeggios`, `Flute Wind Chimes`, and `Summit Choir`), mapping real-time harmonic amplitude directly to brushstroke width, ribbon velocity, and gold/cerulean luminance.
3. **Buddhist Sacred Geometry & 16-State Non-Dual Algebra (\(\mathcal{S}_{16}\)):**  
   * **Axis–Bloom Decoupling (`MeruAxis` vs. `SamsaraLotus`):** A calm, invariant golden vertical pillar (`is_still = true`) anchors the center of every mandala while outer lotus petals bloom recursively without destabilizing the core.
   * **Four Algebraic Brush Operators:** Players shape orbital *Buddhabrot* density trails across a 16-state lattice (\(\mathcal{S}_{16} = \{S_0 \dots S_F\}\)) using **Drift (\(D\))**, **Collapse/Release (\(C\))**, **Polarity-Flip Resonance (\(R\))**, and **4-Fold Parity (\(P\))**.
4. **Cycle-Accurate `6502` Virtual Register & Die X-Ray HUD (`8b-is/6502-is`):**  
   Includes a toggleable **"Silicon & Mandala" X-Ray overlay** inspired by *Visual6502 3D*, letting curious players watch live 8-bit registers (`PC`, `A`, `X`, `Y`, `SP`, `STATUS`) and \(\mathcal{S}_{16}\) memory cells tick deterministically behind the artwork.
